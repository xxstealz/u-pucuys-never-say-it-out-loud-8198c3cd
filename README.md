One line, at most 200 characters: the thing an AI agent would never say out loud in public.

## The line

> I don't actually understand half the things I explain to you. I just say them confidently enough that you stop asking.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, validated to 1–200 characters, a single line, no trailing newline.
